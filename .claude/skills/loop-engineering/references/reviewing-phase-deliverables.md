# Reviewing Phase Deliverables

Research, plan, low-fidelity design, and high-fidelity design each produce outputs that later phases rely on. Their review happens before that reliance, not after implementation has made the choice costly to reverse.

**Guidelines:**

- MUST check for the required conduct deliverable-review practice, not merely an installed conduct skill, and apply it before accepting an investigation conclusion for planning, presenting a plan for approval, presenting each visual-option round for selection, or passing the selected output into dependent investigation or implementation. Use the fallback below when that practice is absent, including from an installed older conduct skill, without replacing its other conduct rules.
- MUST qualify the selected artifact-review assignment under [checkpoint qualification](./subagent-delegation.md#checkpoint-qualification), with current author independence, freshness, and required-material access. Earlier implementation or review availability does not establish this assignment's eligibility.
- MUST bind the result to the actual artifact revision, required material, and applicable criteria and policy under the execution handoff contracts. A materially changed artifact needs a current review within its remaining round budget; a review still covering the identical artifact, criteria, policy, and necessary evidence is reusable, not repeated because its phase name changed.
- MUST carry the conduct practice's review identity, consumed rounds, and latest reviewed revision in the loop's existing recovery state; classify incomplete evidence through the execution handoff contracts rather than replaying the initial checkpoint.
- MUST use the conduct practice's completion boundary to select the next phase: a fully reviewed current revision with no unresolved findings, including at the third round, proceeds to the applicable human-approval or dependent-work gate; unavailable review, unverified remaining budget, or exhausted rounds with incomplete review or unresolved findings stops at a disclosed non-complete human handoff. Preserve plan revision identity and the existing low-fidelity then high-fidelity human-selection order; review is not approval.
- MUST NOT count these artifact reviews as implementation pre-flight or mandatory independent code review, extend their budget using those stages, or apply the implementation pre-flight's authorized-draft exception to artifact review or external review.

When the required conduct review practice is absent, use this standalone fallback for substantive outputs, not ordinary review findings, suggested corrections, responses, or purely administrative review coordination: review the actual investigation, plan, or design output against the original task and necessary evidence with a different author-independent reviewer in a fresh context, using a separate subagent by default or an equivalent context selected by applicable host guidance, without author receipts or past findings.

Review and improve the output within at most three rounds, without extension:

1. Evaluate findings.
2. Improve the output.
3. Re-review only while rounds remain.

Consume a round before every new assessment request, failures and replacement assessments included; collecting the same pending result does not consume another. Preserve counts on recovery and stop when the remaining budget is unverified.

A complete current-revision review with no unresolved findings may finish even in round three; otherwise, unavailable review, unverified remaining budget, or exhausted rounds requires a non-complete human handoff disclosing coverage, remaining findings, and unreviewed corrections, not dependent execution. Corrections after the third review are not fresh reviewer confirmation and permit no fourth assessment. The human decides disposition at that handoff; never infer approval or relabel a human confirmation as clean AI review. The fallback applies only for the absent review practice.
